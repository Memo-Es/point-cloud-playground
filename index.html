Formation is a lie the shader tells for a moment.

Every shape on this page is the same two hundred thousand points. A letter, a sphere, a wave — nothing is ever created or destroyed, only sent somewhere else. The transition is the subject.

01

One field, not three effects

Cursor position, cursor speed and scroll velocity feed a single shared field. The points read it, the light shafts read it, the bloom reads it. Three things reacting to one source behave like a material. Three things each smoothing their own copy of the mouse behave like three gimmicks stacked on a page.

02

Parallax you don't have to fake

These slabs sit at genuinely different depths, so the camera does the parallax for free. The copy holds still while the world keeps moving, because the camera interpolates continuously and the cloud does not — it waits, formed, until you're most of the way through a section.

03

Scroll is not an event stream

Scroll writes numbers into one plain object. The render loop reads that object. Nothing re-renders, nothing re-lays-out, and the canvas never drifts a frame behind the text sitting on top of it.

What's in the build

  • Point atlasAll seven target shapes packed into one float texture. A transition costs two uniform writes, not a multi-megabyte upload mid-scroll.
  • Scatter & reformA burst curve that is zero at both ends, so points land exactly on target no matter how far they fly.
  • Per-point staggerEach point leaves at its own moment. Without it the cloud moves like a slab.
  • Fluid fieldOne smoothed source of disturbance, shared by every layer.
  • Volumetric lightAdditive gradient quads at staggered depths, sheared by the same field.
  • Four GPU tiersChosen at boot from hardware signals, dropped live if the frame budget is missed.
  • Reduced motionHonoured automatically, and overridable by a visible control.
